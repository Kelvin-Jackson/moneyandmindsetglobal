About Money & Mindset Global

Empowering the World to Think Differently About Wealth

Our Story

At Money & Mindset Global, we believe that wealth is more than numbers — it is knowledge, discipline, innovation, and the right mindset. Our platform was created to equip the next generation of thinkers, earners, and entrepreneurs with the tools they need to thrive in a fast-changing world.

We exist to bridge the gap between financial education and real-life application, helping individuals across Africa and beyond understand money, make smarter decisions, and build sustainable wealth with confidence.

Money & Mindset Global was founded by Druscillah Kemunto Omanga, a finance lecturer, researcher, and mentor passionate about empowering students and young professionals. After years of teaching financial literacy, investments, innovation, and entrepreneurship, Drusillah noticed a common pattern:

Many young people are ambitious and talented, but lack access to practical financial guidance and mindset training.

This platform was born out of a desire to change that — to create an open, accessible space where anyone can learn how money works, understand opportunities in innovation, and develop the mindset needed to move from survival to growth.

Today, Money & Mindset Global reaches diverse readers seeking clarity, motivation, and direction on their wealth journeys.

Our Vision

To empower a global generation with the knowledge, mindset, and innovative tools to build sustainable wealth and purpose-driven lives.

Our Mission

To deliver practical financial education, inspire entrepreneurial thinking, and cultivate a confident wealth mindset through accessible insights, research-based content, and global perspectives. We serve students, young professionals, and emerging entrepreneurs seeking long-term financial success.

Meet the Founder

Druscillah Kemunto

Finance Lecturer | Researcher | Entrepreneur | Mentor

Druscillah is an educator committed to shaping financially confident, globally minded individuals. With experience in finance, accounting, innovation, and student mentorship, she brings a unique blend of academic excellence and practical insights to this platform.

Her passion for teaching, combined with her belief in mindset transformation, drives the heartbeat of Money & Mindset Global.
